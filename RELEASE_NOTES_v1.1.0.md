# ExpenseMargin v1.1.0 — Business UX Refresh

This release simplifies ExpenseMargin so it reads and behaves like focused B2B finance software rather than a generic AI SaaS template.

## Marketing refresh
- Reworked the homepage around one clear value proposition and one primary CTA.
- Replaced the decorative alert card with a realistic supplier cost-change product preview.
- Removed gradient-heavy and pill-heavy visual treatment.
- Added a truthful industry-fit band for businesses that buy from suppliers every month.
- Added a three-step workflow: upload invoices → see what changed → protect margin.
- Simplified the pricing page and kept the current v1.0.1 pricing tiers unchanged.

## Application refresh
- Changed the app shell from a dark AI-style sidebar to a light finance-software navigation pattern.
- Grouped navigation into Cost monitoring and Operations.
- Moved Billing and Settings to secondary navigation.
- Shows customer-facing plan names instead of internal plan IDs.
- Simplified dashboard priorities to annual impact, active cost changes, and suppliers monitored.
- Moved uncertain product matches into a dedicated attention bar instead of another KPI card.
- Reduced shadows, radii, gradients, badges, and visual noise throughout shared UI.

## UX research principles
- Borrow interaction and information architecture patterns from established finance/procurement products without copying visual assets, proprietary code, or customer claims.
- Show product value before explaining AI.
- Do not publish unverifiable customer logos or “trusted by” statements. Replace the industry-fit band with verified customer proof once ExpenseMargin has approved references.
